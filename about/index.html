About LetterLinx

LetterLinx is a word game for people who see a jumble of letters and think, “I bet there’s a word in there.” (There is. There are dozens. The clock is ticking.)

How it works

In the main game, you get a grid of 60 letter tiles and 60 seconds. Drag across touching tiles to link them into words. Longer words score far more than short ones, so “CAT” is nice but “CATAPULT” is how you get on the leaderboard.

Every word is checked against a dictionary of more than 270,000 words. If it says your word isn’t real, take it up with the dictionary. We’ve tried. It doesn’t listen.

Two ways to play

  • Linx Daily. The same letters for every player, with a new numbered game each day at midnight. Compare scores on the day’s leaderboard.
  • Brain Booster. A fresh random board every time you play. Chase your all-time best.

Switch between them any time on the Games tab.

Who’s behind it

LetterLinx is a collaboration between Literacy Advocates and Washington, DC-based marketing firm 495 Digital. It started as a side project and turned into a small obsession: a quick game you can play while the coffee brews, and then play eleven more times because the last score was so close.

There’s no big company here. No boardroom, no focus group, no mascot (yet). Just a very long word list, and a firm belief that brains deserve a good workout.

What we care about

  • Fast fun. One round takes a minute. You can stop any time. (You won’t.)
  • Fair play. The letters are random and the dictionary is the same for everyone.
  • Respecting your time and data. We collect only what the game needs. The Privacy Policy explains it in plain English.

Keeping the lights on

LetterLinx is free to play. It’s supported by donations and ads, which pay for hosting and the occasional snack during late-night debugging. Thanks for putting up with the ads. We try to keep them out of the way of your next masterpiece.
