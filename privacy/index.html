Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

Privacy policies are usually where words go to fall asleep. We’ve tried to keep this one awake. It explains what information LetterLinx (www.letterlinx.com) collects, why, and what you can do about it. “We,” “us” and “our” mean Literacy Advocates and 495 Digital, the makers of LetterLinx.

The short version

  • We collect only what the game needs: your account details, your scores and some basic technical data.
  • We show ads from Google AdSense, and use Google Analytics to see how the game is used. Both use cookies.
  • We don’t sell your personal information. Not for money, not for snacks.
  • You can see, change or delete your data any time.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: first name, last name, email address, screen name and password, when you create an account. Your password is stored only in scrambled (hashed) form, so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Email preferences: whether you’ve asked for our weekly stats email and occasional special offers, and when, plus the language you play in so emails arrive in that language.
  • Messages: anything you send us by email, like bug reports or dictionary complaints.

Information the game creates

  • Gameplay data: your scores, the words you found and when you played.
  • Settings and saved progress: your theme, sound and language preferences, the game you last played, and, if you play without an account, your scores and stats. These are saved in your browser’s local storage on your device, so the game remembers you prefer dark mode. If you then create an account or sign in, the scores saved on that device are added to your account.
  • Sign-in: when you sign in, we set a cookie that keeps you signed in on that device for up to 60 days, or until you sign out. Linx WordFinder also sets a cookie with a random device code so each device gets one try per day (see Linx WordFinder below).
  • Reactions: if you send another player a preset reaction (like “Great game!”), they see your screen name, the reaction and which of their scores it was about. We keep reactions for 90 days. You can stop receiving them, or hide them from one player, on the Account screen.
  • Linx WordFinder games: for each daily puzzle you play, your start time, the words you found and when, hints used, your final result, and a nickname if you add one. Signed-in players’ games are linked to their account; guests’ games are linked to a random device code (see Linx WordFinder below).

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: browser type, device type, operating system, IP address, pages visited and time spent. This comes from our hosting provider’s logs and from cookies (see below).
  • Usage data: how the game is used, such as rounds started and finished, scores, the number of words found, which game and language you chose, and when you share or tap a link. This is collected through Google Analytics (see below) and doesn’t include your name or email.

How we use it

  • To run the game, keep your account working and save your scores.
  • To show your screen name and scores on the public leaderboard.
  • To fix bugs and figure out which features people actually use.
  • To reply when you contact us.
  • To send you a weekly email with your stats and the week’s top scores, and occasional special offers, but only if you tick the box to opt in. Every one of these emails has an unsubscribe link, and you can also switch them off on the Account screen. Account emails, like confirmation codes and password resets, are sent whether or not you opt in.
  • To show ads, which keep LetterLinx free.
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse. (Yes, we notice a 9,000-point round.)

What’s public

Your screen name and scores appear on the leaderboard for everyone to admire. Your real name and email do not. Choose a screen name you’re happy for the world to see, since it can’t be changed later.

Linx WordFinder

Linx WordFinder is our daily word hunt, and it works a little differently from the other games:

  • The game is checked on our server. When you play, we store the day’s game on our server: when you started, which words you found and when, how many hints you used, your final time, and whether you finished, ran out of time or ended the game early. This keeps the leaderboard fair (sorry, speed-runners with suspiciously perfect timing).
  • One try a day, even without an account. If you play without signing in, we set a cookie with a random code on your device. It has nothing to do with who you are; it’s how we know that device has already played today’s puzzle, and how we keep its WordFinder stats and streak. We store it only in scrambled (hashed) form, and it lasts about 13 months. If you sign in on that device the same day, that day’s game moves to your account.
  • Nicknames are public. Playing as a guest? You can add a nickname to appear on that day’s leaderboard next to your time. Like a screen name, everyone can see it, so don’t use your real name or anything personal. A nickname can’t match a member’s screen name, and we turn away rude ones.
  • The puzzles are written with help from AI. We use Anthropic’s Claude to help write the topics, words and hints, and every puzzle is reviewed by a person before it goes live. No information about you is sent to Anthropic.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. They’re less delicious than they sound, but they help sites work.

Analytics

LetterLinx uses Google Analytics, through Google Tag Manager, to understand how people use the game, such as which features get used and where players get stuck. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects the usage and technical data described above. You can opt out by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising

LetterLinx uses Google AdSense to show ads. Specifically:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s Ad Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info or, in Europe, www.youronlinechoices.eu.
  • Learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

If you opt out, you’ll still see ads. They just won’t be tailored to you, so prepare for ads about things you have never once wanted.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, we’ll ask for your consent before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used. If you were asked, you can change your choice any time using the “Privacy and cookie settings” link at the bottom of this page.

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as well if you do.

Who we share it with

We don’t sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help run the site: Vercel (hosting), Neon (our database), Resend (sending sign-up and password emails), and Google (AdSense and Analytics). They use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our players or ourselves.
  • If LetterLinx is ever sold or merged, in which case your data would move with it under this same policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and scores for as long as your account exists. Delete your account and we delete your personal information within 30 days, except where the law makes us keep something longer. Server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted. Scores saved only on your device stay there until you clear your browser’s data for this site. Linx WordFinder games played without an account stay on our server as part of that day’s leaderboard history; if you’d like a guest game or nickname removed, email info@letterlinx.com.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit your name and email on the Account screen.
  • Delete your account on the Account screen, or by emailing info@letterlinx.com.
  • Ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct or erase it, by emailing info@letterlinx.com.
  • Stop the weekly stats email and offers with the unsubscribe link in any of them, or by unticking the box on the Account screen.
  • Opt out of personalized ads and of Google Analytics using the links above.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, the UK or California), you may have extra rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA, including the right to object to processing or to complain to your local data protection authority. We’ll honor those requests. We don’t sell personal information as defined by the CCPA.

Donations

Donations go through PayPal. When you tap Donate you leave LetterLinx for PayPal’s site, and PayPal’s own privacy policy covers anything you give them there. We don’t receive your payment details.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, like encrypted connections (HTTPS). No website is 100% secure, though, so please use a password you don’t use anywhere else.

Kids

LetterLinx is a general-audience game and isn’t directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we’ll change the effective date at the top. For big changes, we’ll put a notice on the site. We won’t sneak anything in. That’s not our style.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email info@letterlinx.com. This policy is governed by the laws of the United States of America.
