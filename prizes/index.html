Prizes: Official Rules

Effective date: December 1, 2026

Each month, the top Brain Booster score on LetterLinx wins a prize. These rules explain who can win, how we pick and contact winners, and how prizes are delivered. By playing for a prize, you agree to them. They're written to be read, but they are the official rules.

The short version

  • No purchase is necessary, and buying or donating doesn't improve your chances. Playing is free.
  • To win, you must be a registered LetterLinx member with a confirmed email address, at least 18 years old, and living in the United States.
  • Each month, the member with the highest single-round Brain Booster score in each language (English and Spanish) wins that month's prize.
  • A language’s prize is awarded only if at least 10 eligible members play Brain Booster in that language during the month.
  • Every winner must have a valid US mailing address in their LetterLinx account to receive a prize.
  • The same person, or anyone in the same household (the same mailing address), can’t win two months in a row.
  • We notify winners by email. Digital prizes such as eGift cards and promo codes are delivered by email. Physical prizes are shipped to the winner’s mailing address.

1. Sponsor

The Brain Booster monthly prize (the “Promotion”) is sponsored by Literacy Advocates and 495 Digital, the makers of LetterLinx (“we” or “us”), 350 Beverly Road, Suite 115-450, McLean, VA 22101. Questions: info@letterlinx.com.

2. No purchase necessary

No purchase, payment or donation is required to play or to win, and making one does not increase your chances of winning. The Promotion is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

3. Who can win

  • You must be a registered LetterLinx member who has confirmed their email address. Guest play is not eligible.
  • You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) and a legal resident of the 50 United States or the District of Columbia.
  • Employees, contractors and immediate family members (and people living in the same household) of Literacy Advocates and 495 Digital are not eligible.
  • One account per person. Players found using more than one account are disqualified.
  • To receive a prize, you must have a valid US mailing address in your account (Account screen, “Mailing address”). Adding one is optional for everyone else.

4. Prize months

Each prize month runs from 12:00 a.m. Eastern Time on the 1st day of the month to 11:59:59 p.m. Eastern Time on the last day of that month. The Brain Booster leaderboard starts over at the beginning of each month. The first prize month is December 2026.

5. How winners are chosen

  • The winner for each month and language is the eligible member with the highest score in a single Brain Booster round played during that month, as recorded by our servers.
  • English and Spanish have separate leaderboards, so each language has its own winner.
  • Minimum players: a prize is awarded in a language only if at least 10 eligible members (registered, with a confirmed email address) played at least one scored Brain Booster round in that language during the month. If fewer did, no prize is awarded in that language for that month, and it doesn’t carry over. The game shows how many members have played so far.
  • If two or more members have the same top score, the member who reached that score first wins.
  • No back-to-back wins: a member who won the previous month (in either language) can’t win this month, and neither can a member whose mailing address matches the previous month’s winner’s. We skip them and the next eligible member wins. Addresses are compared after ignoring differences in capitalization, punctuation and common abbreviations (like “St” and “Street”).
  • A member who wins in English can’t also win in Spanish the same month; the Spanish prize goes to the next eligible member.
  • Scores are confirmed by us before a prize is awarded. Our decisions on scores and winners are final.

6. Fair play and disqualification

We may review any score, including the words played, and may disqualify a player and remove their scores if we believe they used software, scripts, automated play, outside help with the game's code, multiple accounts, or any other method that gives an unfair advantage, or broke these rules or the spirit of fair play. If a winner is disqualified or ineligible, the next eligible member on that month's leaderboard becomes the winner.

7. Prizes

  • Each month's prize is shown in the game under “This Month's Prize,” including a description and its approximate retail value where it isn't obvious. Unless stated otherwise, there is one prize per month for each language.
  • Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash, transferred or substituted, except that we may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if the advertised prize becomes unavailable.
  • Gift cards and promo codes are subject to the terms and expiration dates of the company that issues them.
  • A person can win at most one prize per month.
  • Winners are responsible for any taxes on their prize.

8. Winner notification and claiming

  • We notify winners by email, sent to the address on their LetterLinx account, usually within 7 days after the prize month ends.
  • To claim the prize, the winner must, within 14 days of our email, reply to confirm that they meet the eligibility rules and make sure a valid US mailing address is saved in their LetterLinx account. A prize can’t be awarded without one.
  • If we find that a winner shares a mailing address with the previous or following month’s winner, the later win is forfeited and may go to the next eligible member.
  • If a winner doesn't reply in time, can't be reached, or isn't eligible, the prize is forfeited and may be awarded to the next eligible member.
  • We may ask a winner to sign a short statement confirming their eligibility before we send a prize.

9. Prize delivery

  • Digital prizes, such as eGift cards and promo codes, are delivered by email to the winner's account email address, usually within 14 days after the winner claims the prize.
  • Physical prizes are shipped to the US mailing address in the winner’s account, usually within 30 days after it is confirmed. We are not responsible for prizes lost or delayed because of an incorrect address or a problem with the carrier.

10. Publicity

Winners' screen names and winning scores appear in the Monthly Winners list on the Brain Booster leaderboard. We won't publish a winner's real name, email or address without their permission.

11. Privacy

We use your information to run the Promotion: to confirm eligibility, contact winners and deliver prizes. We use the mailing address in your account to send prizes and to apply the household rule. When a prize is claimed, we keep a copy of the address with the win until two months after that prize month ends, then delete it. See our Privacy Policy for details.

  • We are not responsible for lost, late or incomplete game data, or for problems with networks, devices or software that prevent a score from being recorded.
  • To the extent permitted by law, by taking part you release Literacy Advocates and 495 Digital from liability for any injury, loss or damage arising from the Promotion or a prize.
  • The Promotion is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Virginia, United States.

13. Trademarks

Brand names and logos of prizes (for example, gift card brands) belong to their owners. Those companies are not sponsors of, and are not affiliated with or responsible for, this Promotion.

14. Winners list and questions

Recent winners are listed on the Brain Booster leaderboard in the game. For questions about these rules or a prize, email info@letterlinx.com.
