Contact Us

Got something to say? We’d love to hear it. Use as many letters as you like. There’s no 60-second timer on this page.

  • LetterLinx info@letterlinx.com
  • 495 Digital info@495Digital.com
  • Literacy Advocates info@literacy.net

We usually reply within 2–3 business days. If it takes longer, we’re probably stuck on a really good grid.

Good reasons to write in

  • Bug reports. Tiles frozen? Score acting strange? Tell us what device and browser you’re on and what happened just before things went sideways. Screenshots are gold.
  • Dictionary disputes. Think a real word got rejected, or a fake one slipped through? Send it over. We’ll check it, and we’ll admit it when you’re right.
  • Ideas and feedback. New themes, game modes, features you’d love to see. Wild ideas welcome.
  • Account and privacy requests. Want to see, change or delete your data? Email us from the address on your account and we’ll handle it. (You can also delete your account yourself on the Account screen.)
  • Ads. If an ad looks inappropriate or broken, let us know where you saw it.
  • Fan mail. Not required, but it does make our day.

Before you hit send

Please don’t email us your password. We’ll never ask for it, and we’d rather not know that it’s “password123.”
