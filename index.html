    About LetterLinx

    LetterLinx is a free word game you play in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Link neighbouring letter tiles to spell words, score more for longer words, and see how many points you can make in 60 seconds. Play in English or Spanish. A new numbered game starts every day at midnight with the same letters for every player. Save your scores and climb the day's leaderboard.

    How do you play LetterLinx?

    Tap GO, then drag across neighbouring tiles (across, up, down or diagonally) to spell a word of 2 or more letters. Lift your finger to play it. Used tiles disappear, the letters above slide down and new letters drop in at the top.

    How is the score worked out?

    By word length: 2 letters score 1 point, 3 letters 3, 4 letters 5, 5 letters 10, 6 letters 15, 7 letters 25, 8 letters 35, and 9 or more letters 50.

    Is LetterLinx free?

    Yes. You can play without an account. A free account saves your score history and puts you on the leaderboard.

    Can I play in Spanish?

    Yes. Choose Español in Settings to play Letra Linx, with Spanish letters including Ñ, a Spanish dictionary and its own leaderboard.
